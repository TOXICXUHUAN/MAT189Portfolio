    Project Full Title: Wood Lantern with 3D Printed Handle
    Short Name: lantern
    Completed Date: August 1st 2024
    Medium: Laser cut plywood, 3D printing filament
    Course Info: MAT 111PF - PROTOTYPE-FABRICATE by MANOUDAKI N
    Abstract: This is a lantern made mostly out of plywood with laser cut patterns. The patterns are of a alternating sequence between a bird and some geometric shapes. The inside has a 3D printed candle/LED holder. I first designed the patterns in Fusion360 and then tested out various designs by using the actual laser cutter and seeing which one works best. Then I modeled the handle and the candle holder to fit the notches I made for the panels. Then I laser cut the panels and 3D printed the handle and assembled them.
